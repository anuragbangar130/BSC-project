A B.Sc. Computer Science Learning and Information Platform

About BCS Pedia

BCS Pedia is a Wikipedia-style educational platform designed for B.Sc. Computer Science students. It provides subject-wise educational information and learning resources in one place.

Main Aim

The main aim of BCS Pedia is to provide students with an organized and easy-to-access platform for learning, sharing and managing Computer Science related educational content.

What BCS Pedia Provides

Subject-wise Learning

Educational information is organized according to subjects for easy learning and access.

Learning Information

Students can access useful notes, explanations and educational information.

Multiple File Formats

Supports documents, PDFs, images, audio, video and question papers.

NEP 2024 & Autonomous

The platform supports different academic patterns including NEP 2024 and Autonomous.

Teacher Contribution

Verified teachers can add, update and manage educational information.

Activity Tracking

Important content activities such as add, update and delete are tracked for administration.

Who Can Use BCS Pedia?

Students: Access and learn from subject-wise educational information.

Teachers: Contribute and manage educational information after verification.

Administrators: Manage users, teachers, subjects and educational content.
